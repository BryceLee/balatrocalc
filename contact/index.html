Contact BalatroCalc

Contact the independent BalatroCalc site operator for calculator issues, guide corrections, paid feature support, privacy requests, or rights concerns.

Email

support@balatrocalc.com

This address is for BalatroCalc website support. We cannot provide support on behalf of the Balatro developer, publisher, or a game storefront. Response time depends on the request; this page does not promise a fixed turnaround.

Report a score mismatch

Include the page URL, game version, deck and stake, hand level, played and held cards, Joker order and counters, expected score, and the calculator result. A screenshot of the relevant Breakdown steps is helpful. Explain which effect first differs from the game so we can reproduce the problem.

Billing and access

For paid feature questions, include the feature name, purchase date, and transaction reference if available. Send purchase details only by email, not in a public report. Never send passwords, complete payment card numbers, or a PayPal login. See Terms for the applicable service terms.

Corrections, privacy, and rights

For an article correction, link the page and identify the sentence or example. For a privacy request, describe the data or account involved without including unnecessary sensitive information. Read the Privacy Policy for our data practices. For a rights concern, identify the material and your relationship to the rights holder.
