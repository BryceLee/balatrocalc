How AI Credits work

AI responses have a real token cost, so Credits are separate from every existing BalatroCalc membership, including Seed Pro. Usage is calculated from the selected model, input and output tokens, and conversation context. The exact deduction appears after each answer and in your usage history.

AI Credit packages are one-time digital purchases and are non-refundable after purchase, except where applicable law requires otherwise or a verified duplicate or incorrect charge must be corrected. Start with the 30-Credit package if you want to test the service before buying more.
